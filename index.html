How I work

I start with how the business actually works: who touches an order, where it gets written down, what goes wrong most often. Then I build the simplest thing that fixes it.

I deploy and maintain what I build.

Kirana has been running on an AWS EC2 instance behind nginx, supervised by PM2, since the day I shipped it. When it misbehaves, I'm the one who gets the call. Most people skip that part, and it's the part that decides whether software is an asset or a liability.

Sometimes the right answer isn't custom software at all. Some of my work is WordPress, because an owner who wants to change prices on a Sunday night needs a tool they can run themselves. On the print platform I left out online payments on purpose. The shop invoices its customers, so that feature would have been for my portfolio, not for them.

You own everything I build: the domain, the source code, the database and the records. No retainer, no licence, no dependency on me.

Currently DevOps Lead at ZeezTech, a product engineering studio in Hyderabad. Day to day I work in TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL and Docker.
