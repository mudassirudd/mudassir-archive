About

How I work

I start with how the business actually works — who touches an order, where it gets written down, what goes wrong most often — and then build the least complicated thing that fixes it.

I deploy and maintain what I build. Kirana has been running on an AWS EC2 instance behind nginx, supervised by PM2, since I shipped it. I'm the one who gets the call when it misbehaves. That's the part most people skip, and it's the part that decides whether software is an asset or a liability.

Sometimes the right answer isn't custom software at all. Some of my work is WordPress, because an owner who needs to change prices on a Sunday night is better served by a tool they can operate than a bespoke one they can't. And on the print platform I deliberately left out online payments, because the shop invoices — that feature would have been for my portfolio, not for them.

You own everything I build: domain, source code, database, records. No retainer, no licence, no dependency on me.

Currently DevOps Lead at ZeezTech, a product engineering studio in Hyderabad — working day to day in TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL and Docker.
